# Brand name research, round 4 (surname-led: "Malhi")
Date: 19 Oct 2026. Founder: Harshmeet Malhi (Punjab). Roadmap: rider app (navigation, group rides, SOS, community) -> smart bike/helmet devices -> own electric bike. Must work in India and globally. Rounds 1-3 names are not reused.

## Read this first (honest caveats)
- **This is NOT a legal trademark search.** IP India, WIPO, USPTO, EUIPO were not searched. Have a trademark attorney check Classes 9, 12, 25, 35, 42 (exact and phonetic) before buying or filing.
- **Domains:** .com via Verisign RDAP, .in via rdap.org (redirects to the .in registry; the NIXI host I first tried returned a web page, not RDAP, so I discarded that run). 404 = not registered, 200 = registered. 19 Oct 2026. "Free" is not a guarantee (premium/reserved, or taken any minute). .co, social handles, App Store/Play Store NOT checked. Nothing bought.
- **Web checks:** 1-2 WebSearch queries per name; absence of hits is weak evidence. Language/meaning notes are my own reading; have Punjabi and Hindi speakers say each name aloud.
- Context: "Mali" is a country (Africa) and "Malhi" is a common Jat Sikh/Punjabi surname, so a hit-free search for the plain word is not possible.

## Key finding: plain "Malhi" is crowded and not ownable
- **Malhi Motors already exists**: at least two Hero MotoCorp dealerships in Ludhiana, Punjab (Chander Nagar; Civil Lines/Hargovind Pura, listed as Malhi Motors / Hero Malhi Motors) with Google/Hero listings and reviews. Same state as the founder, same industry. "Malhi Motors" is out.
- **RideBoom** (bike taxi / e-rickshaw) is led by CEO Harminder "Harry" Malhi (PR.com 2024): another mobility business with a Malhi in public view, though not a Malhi-named brand.
- malhi.com is registered; malhi.in is registered. malhimotors.com is registered. MLH Motors is a Turkish retail business and "MLH" is Major League Hacking (a well-known developer brand, tech/app overlap): "MLH" is out.
- **Is a surname brand protectable?** In general (not legal advice): a surname alone is typically treated as weak or non-distinctive in many systems (e.g. USPTO treats a primarily-merely-a-surname mark as needing proof of acquired distinctiveness; India's Trade Marks Act s.9/s.11 and EU practice also scrutinise common surnames). A very common surname like Malhi is weaker still, and other Malhis (like the Ludhiana dealers) can use their own name honestly. Registration is sometimes possible with a distinctive logo/stylisation, but you could not stop other Malhi-named vehicle businesses. **A coined variant is safer**: it is more distinctive, easier to register and enforce, and not a country/surname collision. Trade-off: a coined word loses some of the "founder's name" story, though it can keep it ("from Malhi").
- Practical advice: do not use plain Malhi, Malhi Motors, or MLH. Prefer a coined Malhi-sound word, and keep the surname as a story/credit ("by Malhi" / "Made by Harshmeet Malhi").

### Meaning, pronunciation, connotations (own linguistic judgement, not native-speaker verified)
- Likely pronunciation: "MUL-hee-vah" (Punjabi Malhi) or "MAL-hee-vah". Expect ambiguity on the first vowel and on the "h".
- English: no meaning. It reads as a soft, invented name. The "Mal-" start can echo "mal-" (bad) subliminally.
- Hindi/Punjabi: "Malhi" is a Jat Sikh surname, so the name carries the founder's identity. The "-iva" ending is also the Sanskrit "iva" (like, as if), so "Malhiva" can be read as "like Malhi". It has no negative meaning. It is close to Malviya, a Brahmin surname, which can cause mix-ups.
- Spanish/Portuguese/French: "mal" means bad or ill, and French "malheur" means misfortune. Malhiva visually evokes it slightly but does not mean it. Low risk. In Portuguese "malhar" is to work out or beat, which is harmless here.
- Arabic: no known meaning. It is pronounceable (مالهيفا). Not verified with a native speaker.
- Chinese (e.g. 马尔希瓦), Japanese (マルヒバ): no meaning, easy to transliterate. German: no meaning, pronounced "Mal-hee-va".
- Hebrew: "malhiv" (מלהיב) means exciting, a positive accidental echo.

### Short-form spelling check
- Spelling traps: Malheeva, Malhiwa, Malhivaa, Malviya, Malhiva with 'v' read as 'w' (Hindi/Punjabi speakers). Reserve at least .com, .in, and consider malhiwa.com and malviya-like typos only if confusion shows up in testing.
- Short forms: "Malhi" (too generic and taken by the surname), "MHV" (weak), "Malhi-V". Spoken, the 3-syllable name is fine. Test it by having 8-10 people write it down after hearing it.

### Verdict and risks
**Verdict: Promising. Domains and X/YouTube look free, and nothing in the ride/vehicle space uses the name.** Cautious go, pending checks.
Risks:
1. Not a trademark search. Class 9 (apps), 12 (vehicles), 9/25 (helmets, apparel) and 42 (software) still need IP India and WIPO searches, plus an attorney.
2. Domain results for .co and .io are low confidence. Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn are unchecked.
3. Phonetic similarity to Maliva (app) and Malviya (surname) can cause confusion and search misses.
4. Pronunciation and spelling variation (Malhi/Malhiva, v/w).
Next steps: confirm domains at a registrar (.com and .in first), check IG/FB/LinkedIn manually, run the trademark searches, and do the say-and-spell test.
