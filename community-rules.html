RiderKit community rules

Version 1. The community is for riders aged 18 or over. Plain-language starter rules, not legal advice; have them reviewed before a public launch. Contact for reports and complaints: [CONTACT EMAIL / GRIEVANCE OFFICER].

Not allowed

  • Harassment, hate or threats towards any person or group.
  • Nudity or sexual content, graphic violence or injuries.
  • Dangerous riding: promoting stunts, racing or speeding on public roads, riding without a helmet, drunk riding.
  • Personal information: number plates, home addresses, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, other people's photos without consent. Photos can show your house or plate: check before you post.
  • Spam, scams, ads, links: external links, phone numbers and e-mail addresses are blocked on purpose.
  • Illegal activity, or content that is not yours to share.

Your controls

  • Your profile is private by default. Locations are never part of a post: only an outline picture (start and end hidden by default) and summary numbers.
  • Report any post, comment, message, profile or club from its menu. Block any rider: they cannot see you, follow you or message you, and are not told.
  • Choose who may message you (nobody / followers / anyone). You can delete your posts and comments, leave clubs, and delete your account (everything you created is removed).

What happens when something is reported

Content reported by 3 different riders is hidden automatically until a moderator looks. Moderators can remove content and suspend accounts. We aim to review reports within 72 hours (this is a small team). Direct messages are private, but when you report one, the reported message and a few before it are shared with moderators.
