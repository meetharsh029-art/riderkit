Share live location with family

Family can open the link without installing anything. They see only your latest position, a short trail, your name and when it ends; they cannot see anything else or track you after it expires or you stop. Anyone who has the link can view it, so send it only to people you trust. Needs RiderKit open with GPS on (phones pause GPS in the background, so the link may show an older position).

Tyre tracking

Enter when each tyre was fitted. Check intervals are generic guidance (pressure weekly, inspect every 1,000 km, replace-by guide 25,000 km / 5 years) – follow your manual and tyre maker.

Documents

Reminders

Covers dated items only: document expiry (insurance, PUC, licence, RC), service items with a month/day interval, tyre age and pressure checks. Service due only by km cannot be reminded by date. Push needs the RiderKit server to be awake; if it is not, reminders show as soon as you open the app.
Alerts also appear in the app when a document is due within 30 days or expired.

Group ride LIVE

No account. Your live location is shared with everyone who has the code, only while you are in the group. The group stays open as long as anyone is still on their trip. It closes when the admin cancels it or every rider has ended their trip, and your position is deleted from the server then. Keep this screen open: browsers pause GPS when the phone is locked.

    Riders

    Trip controls

    End my trip: you stop sharing and your pin disappears; the group keeps going for riders still out, and you can rejoin with the code. The group closes when the admin cancels it or every rider has ended their trip. If the admin ends their trip or leaves, the next rider becomes admin.
    Breakdown / SOS: tap Alert at the top, pick what happened, and every rider sees it with your location. Alerts only show while RiderKit is open on their screen – a web page cannot ring or vibrate on a locked phone. For real emergencies call 112.

    Hazards & places

    Move or zoom the map to load pins.

    Add a pin

    Pins are added by riders like you. They are anonymous, unverified and may be wrong or out of date: always ride to the conditions in front of you. Hazards expire in 14 days unless riders confirm them; places last about 6 months. Pins that three riders report are hidden. Limits: 5 new pins per hour.

    SOS & settings

    Account (optional)

    You can use RiderKit fully as a guest. An account only adds sign-in on this and other devices. Terms · Privacy · Delete account

    Backup & restore

    One JSON file with all your rides (incl. photos), vehicles, service, tyres, documents, contacts, fuel log, stops and settings. Stored only on this device until you save the file somewhere. The file is not encrypted.
    About RiderKit
    RiderKit is not a certified safety device. Crash alert is experimental and may miss a crash or trigger falsely. Your data stays on this device.
    Privacy policy
    Alert is sent as a pre-filled message that you/the rider must press Send on. The app cannot send silently from a browser.
