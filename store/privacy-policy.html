Summary

  • No accounts. No sign-up or login.
  • We do not sell your data. Apart from optional group rides and feedback reports (described below), nothing leaves your device.
  • Your rides, notes, photos, service reminders and emergency-contact details are stored only on your device.

Information the app uses, and why

  • Location (GPS) – used while you are recording a ride, to draw your route and compute distance and speed, and to put your last known position in an emergency message. Ride routes are saved on your device only.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer) – used only when you switch on "Crash alert", to detect a possible impact. Readings are analysed on the device in real time and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Microphone – used only while you hold the "Hold to talk" button in a group ride, to record a short voice clip (up to 15 seconds) for the riders in your group. The app never listens in the background and never records unless the button is held. You can refuse the permission; quick messages and everything else still work.
  • Photos – only photos you choose to attach to a ride are used. They are stored on your device inside the app's storage and are not uploaded.
  • Emergency-contact data (your name, contact name, phone number, preferred messaging app) – entered by you, saved on your device, and used only to pre-fill an emergency message.
  • Notes, odometer and service reminders – entered by you and stored on your device.

Feedback and bug reports (optional)

If you choose "Report a bug / send feedback", the app sends to our server: the type you picked (bug, idea or other), your message, the contact details only if you type them, and technical info to help us fix problems: app version, browser/device description (user-agent string), platform, language, screen size, whether the app is installed, and whether you were online. Your location is never included, and we do not store your IP address (it is used only briefly to limit abuse). Reports are stored in a text file on our server, read only by the app owner, and are used only to fix bugs and improve the app. Please do not put sensitive personal information in a report. We delete reports when they are no longer needed; to ask for deletion of a report, contact us at the address above. If you are offline, the report is kept on your device and sent later.

Group ride messages and voice clips

In a group ride you can send one-tap quick messages (for example "Stop", "Fuel", "Need help") and push-to-talk voice clips to the other riders in your group. Quick messages include your rider name and the message, and the most recent ones are kept in the server's memory only while the group is open. A voice clip is uploaded to our server and relayed to the members of your group. Voice clips are transient: they are held in the server's memory only, are never written to disk or a database, are deleted automatically after a few minutes (or sooner if the group closes or the server restarts), and are limited in length, size and number per group. Only members of your group can fetch a clip. We do not listen to, transcribe, analyse or keep voice clips, and no copy is stored on your device by the app. Messages are shown only while RiderKit is open on the other riders' screens. Anyone who holds your group code can join the group and hear the clips sent while they are a member, so share the code only with people you ride with.

Emergency messages

If a crash alert is not cancelled, the app opens your phone's SMS or WhatsApp app with a message already written to your emergency contact, including a Google Maps link to your last known location. The app does not send it by itself – you must press Send in that app. Once you send it, the message is handled by your messaging provider under its own terms; RiderKit does not see or store it.

Map tiles (third party)

The map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org). To fetch them, your device's request necessarily reveals your IP address and the map area (tile coordinates) being viewed to OpenStreetMap, which is operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. We do not receive this information. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. Tiles you have viewed may be cached on your device for offline use.

Sharing, selling, advertising

We do not share your data with anyone, do not sell it, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual rides and reminders inside the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes all RiderKit data from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data.

Children

RiderKit is intended for people old enough to ride a motorcycle legally and is not directed at children.

Safety notice

RiderKit is not a certified safety or medical device. Crash alert is experimental and may fail to detect a crash or may trigger by mistake. Do not rely on it for your safety.
