Summary

  • Accounts are optional (see below); guests can use every feature.
  • We do not sell your data. Apart from the optional features described below (group rides, hazard pins, live-share link, reminder notifications, feedback reports, accounts), nothing leaves your device except requests to the free map, routing, search and weather services.
  • Your rides, notes, photos, service reminders and emergency-contact details are stored only on your device.

Information the app uses, and why

  • Location (GPS) – used while you are recording a ride, to draw your route and compute distance and speed, and to put your last known position in an emergency message. Ride routes are saved on your device only.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer) – used only when you switch on "Crash alert", to detect a possible impact. Readings are analysed on the device in real time and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Microphone – used only while you hold the "Hold to talk" button in a group ride, to record a short voice clip (up to 15 seconds) for the riders in your group. The app never listens in the background and never records unless the button is held. You can refuse the permission; quick messages and everything else still work.
  • Photos – only photos you choose to attach to a ride are used. They are stored on your device inside the app's storage and are not uploaded.
  • Emergency-contact data (your name, contact name, phone number, preferred messaging app) – entered by you, saved on your device, and used only to pre-fill an emergency message.
  • Notes, odometer and service reminders – entered by you and stored on your device.

Feedback and bug reports (optional)

If you choose "Report a bug / send feedback", the app sends to our server: the type you picked (bug, idea or other), your message, the contact details only if you type them, and technical info to help us fix problems: app version, browser/device description (user-agent string), platform, language, screen size, whether the app is installed, and whether you were online. Your location is never included, and we do not store your IP address (it is used only briefly to limit abuse). Reports are stored in a text file on our server, read only by the app owner, and are used only to fix bugs and improve the app. Please do not put sensitive personal information in a report. We delete reports when they are no longer needed; to ask for deletion of a report, contact us at the address above. If you are offline, the report is kept on your device and sent later.

Accounts (optional)

You can use RiderKit without an account. If you create one, we store on our server: your email address (or phone number, if phone sign-in is offered), a salted scrypt hash of your password (we never store or see the password itself), the date you signed up, and for each signed-in device a hashed session token with a short device description (truncated user-agent) and last-use time. Verification and reset codes are stored only as one-way hashes and expire after 10 minutes. We email you (or SMS you, for phone sign-in) only the codes you request and a notice if someone tries to register your address. These messages are sent through our email provider (and SMS provider, if enabled), which therefore processes your address/number and the message to deliver it. We do not use your email or phone for marketing and do not sell or share it. Your rides, notes and photos remain on your device and are not part of your account. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and your IP address is used only briefly in memory for that.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings (SOS & settings) → Account → Delete account. On the web, without the app: open delete-account.html on our website, enter your email and password, and confirm. If you cannot sign in, use "Forgot password" first, or email us at the contact address above from the registered address and we will delete the account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account record, email/phone, password hash, all sign-ins and pending codes are removed from our server. Retention: we keep nothing about a deleted account, except that copies may remain for a short time in the hosting provider's routine backups (if any), and our email/SMS provider may keep delivery logs for a limited period under its own policy. Group-ride data is never tied to an account and is already deleted automatically (see below).

Group ride messages and voice clips

In a group ride you can send one-tap quick messages (for example "Stop", "Fuel", "Need help") and push-to-talk voice clips to the other riders in your group. Quick messages include your rider name and the message, and the most recent ones are kept in the server's memory only while the group is open. A voice clip is uploaded to our server and relayed to the members of your group. Voice clips are transient: they are held in the server's memory only, are never written to disk or a database, are deleted automatically after a few minutes (or sooner if the group closes or the server restarts), and are limited in length, size and number per group. Only members of your group can fetch a clip. We do not listen to, transcribe, analyse or keep voice clips, and no copy is stored on your device by the app. Messages are shown only while RiderKit is open on the other riders' screens. Anyone who holds your group code can join the group and hear the clips sent while they are a member, so share the code only with people you ride with.

Reminder notifications (optional)

If you tap Turn on reminders (Documents screen), your browser creates a push subscription (an address and keys issued by your browser's push service, e.g. Google FCM, Mozilla or Apple). We send the RiderKit server that subscription, your time zone offset, and a short list of dated reminders (a label such as "PUC expiry" or a service item name, and its due date). We use it only to send you notifications near those dates. No name, e-mail, location or vehicle details are sent. The data is deleted when you turn reminders off, when the push service reports the subscription gone, or 400 days after your last sync. Notification text passes encrypted through your browser vendor's push service. Without turning this on, reminders are only shown inside the app.

Hazard and place pins (optional)

The "Hazards & places" screen shows pins added by other riders (potholes, puncture shops, mechanics, dhabas, fuel stations) and lets you add, confirm, mark as gone, or report pins. When you add a pin we store its type, its position (rounded to about 1 m), your optional short note (links and phone numbers are removed), vote counts, and an expiry time. Your votes and pins are linked only to a random device identifier generated in your browser; the server keeps a salted hash of it to stop double voting and limit spam, never the raw value, your name, or your IP address (your IP is used in memory only for rate limiting). Anyone can see a pin and its note, so do not write personal information. Hazard pins expire after 14 days and place pins after about 6 months unless riders confirm them; pins are removed when riders vote them gone and hidden when three riders report them. To have a pin removed, report it in the app or contact us. Pins you view are cached on your device for offline use.

Routing, place search and weather (third parties)

When you use these features your device (not our server) contacts free public services and they see your IP address plus what you send: OSRM (router.project-osrm.org) receives your current position and the destination to plan a route, and again when you leave the route and the app asks for a new one (automatic re-routing, at most once per 25 seconds); Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) receives the place name you search for; Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) receives a few coordinates along your route or at your location to get the forecast; OpenStreetMap tile servers receive the map tiles you view. Spoken turn prompts use your device's own text-to-speech; on some phones the browser's speech engine may process text through the device vendor's speech service. These requests are made only when you use the feature.

Live location sharing link (optional)

If you tap Start sharing on the Ride screen, RiderKit creates a link and sends your GPS position (about every 5 seconds), accuracy, speed and the optional name you typed to our server while sharing is on. Anyone who has the link can see your latest position, a trail of up to about 60 recent points (one every 15 seconds or more), your name and the time the link ends; they need no account. Choose how long it lasts (1 to 12 hours). This data is held in server memory only, is never written to disk or a database, and is deleted when you stop sharing, when the link expires, or if the server restarts. The link contains a random, unguessable code, but anyone you give it to can pass it on, so share it only with people you trust. Your own copy of the secret key stays on your device. We do not keep a history of where you have been.

Group expenses and stops

Inside a live group ride, riders can record shared expenses (amount, a short label, who paid, who shares it) and request or plan stops (type, optional note, and a position). These are visible to everyone in the group, are held in server memory only (never written to disk), and are deleted when the group ends or expires. RiderKit only does the arithmetic; it does not process payments or ask for bank or UPI details.

Ride recap card

The recap card is drawn on your device from a saved ride (distance, time, speeds, and a plain outline of the route with no map or street names). By default the first and last 300 m are left out so your home is not revealed. Nothing is uploaded: the image only leaves your device if you choose Share or Save.

Emergency messages

If a crash alert is not cancelled, the app opens your phone's SMS or WhatsApp app with a message already written to your emergency contact, including a Google Maps link to your last known location. The app does not send it by itself – you must press Send in that app. Once you send it, the message is handled by your messaging provider under its own terms; RiderKit does not see or store it.

Map tiles (third party)

The map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org). To fetch them, your device's request necessarily reveals your IP address and the map area (tile coordinates) being viewed to OpenStreetMap, which is operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. We do not receive this information. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. Tiles you have viewed may be cached on your device for offline use.

RiderKit keeps a small cache (up to 600 tiles) of the map tiles you have actually viewed on your device, to make revisits faster and let those places show without signal. It never downloads map areas in advance. You can delete it in SOS & settings → Map cache.

Sharing, selling, advertising

We do not share your data with anyone, do not sell it, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual rides and reminders inside the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes all RiderKit data from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data.

Children

RiderKit is intended for people old enough to ride a motorcycle legally and is not directed at children.

Safety notice

RiderKit is not a certified safety or medical device. Crash alert is experimental and may fail to detect a crash or may trigger by mistake. Do not rely on it for your safety.

Community (profiles, posts, photos, clubs, chats)

If you join the optional community (18+ only, confirmed by you at sign-up) we store: your chosen handle, display name, bio, avatar and vehicle text; your visibility and message settings; your consent record (time and rules version); posts (caption, summary ride numbers such as distance and duration, an outline picture of your route with the first and last 300 m hidden by default, and up to 3 photos); comments, likes, who you follow and who follows you, clubs you belong to and chat messages in them, direct messages, your blocks, and reports you file. We never publish GPS points, start/end coordinates or your email address. Photos are re-encoded on your device and the server also removes metadata (EXIF, including GPS) before storing them. Photos can still show places or number plates: check before posting.

Who sees what: profiles are private by default; posts follow the audience you choose; direct messages are visible to the two people involved and, if a message is reported, to moderators (the reported message and a few before it). Moderators can see reported content and the operator can technically read stored content (it is not end-to-end encrypted).

Retention and deletion: community content stays until you delete it or delete your account; deleting the account removes your profile, handle, posts and photos, comments, likes, follows, club memberships (clubs you own are transferred or deleted), chat and direct messages you wrote, blocks and the reports you filed. Reports other people filed about you are kept in minimal form (reason and date, without content) for abuse handling. You can download your data from Community → Me. Chat history is trimmed automatically (oldest messages are dropped after a few hundred per room).
